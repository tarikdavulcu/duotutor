How do I switch postures?

DuoTutor follows the hinge on iPhone Duo automatically. On any device you can use the posture switcher at the bottom of the screen: Closed, Open or Tabletop.

How do two people play?

Choose Tabletop and tap Start match. The tutor keeps the half with the answer key; the learner's half rotates 180° in Face-to-Face mode. Use V-Tent if you stand the device up.

Why doesn't pronunciation grading work?

Grading runs fully on your device. If your language's on-device dictation model isn't installed, download it in Settings › General › Keyboard › Dictation, then try again.

What is free?

Solo study is always free. Your first 3 complete Duo sessions are free with every feature. After that you can buy Pro Lifetime once, or subscribe to the Teacher Pack.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the paywall (graduation-cap icon) and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel the Teacher Pack?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › DuoTutor. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. Read the privacy policy.
